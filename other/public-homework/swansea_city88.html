Swansea City A.F.C. — 88 Facts

A concise list of 88 facts about Swansea City Association Football Club. Made by Josiah.

  1. Swansea City A.F.C. was founded in 1912 as Swansea Town.
  2. The club changed its name to Swansea City A.F.C. in 1970.
  3. Swansea is based in Swansea, Wales.
  4. Nickname: The Swans.
  5. Another nickname: The Jacks.
  6. Club colors: White and black.
  7. Club motto: “Ymlaen y Cob” (Forward the Swans).
  8. Official website: swanseacity.com
  9. First ground: Vetch Field (1912–2005).
  10. Current ground: Swansea.com Stadium (formerly Liberty Stadium), opened 2005.
  11. Stadium capacity: 21,000.
  12. Record attendance: 32,796 vs. Arsenal, 17 February 1968.
  13. Premier League best finish: 8th, 2014–15.
  14. Football League Division 1 best finish: 6th, 1981–82.
  15. Championship play-off winners: 2010–11.
  16. Football League Division 3 champions: 1999–2000.
  17. Football League Division 4 champions: 1976–77.
  18. Football League Cup winners: 2012–13.
  19. Welsh Cup winners: 11 times.
  20. Welsh Cup runners-up: 8 times.
  21. Football League Trophy winners: 1994 and 2006.
  22. European Cup Winners’ Cup qualifications: multiple occasions during the club's history.
  23. Europa League participation: 2013–14 (following the 2013 League Cup win).
  24. Record win: 12–0 vs. Sliema Wanderers, 15 September 1982.
  25. Record league win: 8–1 vs. Bristol Rovers, 15 April 1922.
  26. Record FA Cup win (notable big score): 8–1 vs. Notts County.
  27. Record European win: 12–0 vs. Sliema Wanderers, 1982.
  28. Record home win: 12–0 vs. Sliema Wanderers, 1982.
  29. Record away win: 6–4 vs. Bradford City, 23 November 1990.
  30. Record league defeat: 8–1 vs. Fulham, 22 January 1938.
  31. Record FA Cup defeat: 8–0 vs. Liverpool, 9 January 1990.
  32. Record European defeat: 8–0 vs. AS Monaco, 1 October 1991.
  33. Record home defeat: 6–1 vs. Bradford Park Avenue, 14 September 1946.
  34. Record away defeat: 8–0 vs. Liverpool, 9 January 1990.
  35. Most appearances: Wilfred Milne – 586 matches (club record).
  36. Most goals (all-time): Ivor Allchurch – 166 goals for the club.
  37. Most league goals in a season: Cyril Pearce – 35 goals (1931–32).
  38. Most international caps while at club: Ashley Williams (Wales).
  39. First Welsh club to play in the English Football League system in the 20th century.
  40. First promotion to the top flight (old Division 1): achieved in 1981.
  41. First Premier League promotion: 2010–11 season (play-off winners).
  42. First Premier League goalscorer for the club: Scott Sinclair.
  43. First Premier League manager: Brendan Rodgers.
  44. First FA Cup semi-final appearance: 1925.
  45. First League Cup final appearance: 2013 (and winners that year).
  46. First European competition appearance: European Cup Winners’ Cup, 1961.
  47. Club mascot name: Sammy the Swan.
  48. Club anthem commonly sung by fans: “Swansea, Swansea”.
  49. Record transfer fee paid: ~£5.5 million for Wilfried Bony.
  50. Record transfer fee received: ~£25 million for Michu.
  51. Vetch Field hosted the club from 1912 until 2005.
  52. Last match at Vetch Field took place in 2005.
  53. First match at the Swansea.com Stadium: 2005.
  54. First televised match involving Swansea: historic broadcasts in the 1960s.
  55. First match in the European Cup Winners’ Cup: early 1960s.
  56. Longest unbeaten league run: around 20 matches in club history.
  57. Longest losing run: double-digit losing streaks in some seasons.
  58. First manager: Walter Whittaker.
  59. Longest-serving manager (early-mid 20th century): Haydn Green.
  60. First player capped by Wales while at the club: Ivor Allchurch.
  61. Academy graduates include Ashley Williams and Joe Allen.

  63. Secondary rivalry: Newport County.
